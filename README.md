# Spray Factory / 喷涂工厂

![喷涂工厂混带生产线](docs/images/factory.jpg)

使用喷涂机作为万能工厂，享受无需分拣器的工厂设计，并处理混入混出所带来的全新挑战。

工作功耗 300 kW，待机 10 kW。随一级传送带解锁；制造需 4 个铁块、2 个齿轮、2 个电路板，基础时间 3 秒。默认快捷入口位于建造菜单的仓储区第 4 个位置。

## English

Turn the spray coater into a universal factory. Build production lines without sorters and take on the challenges of mixed inputs and outputs.

Working power: 300 kW. Idle power: 10 kW. Unlocked alongside Conveyor Belt Mk.I. Craft one factory from 4 iron ingots, 2 gears and 2 circuit boards in 3 seconds. By default, its build-menu shortcut is the fourth slot in the Storage category.
