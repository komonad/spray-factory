# Changelog

## 0.1.1

- 支持最多 6 种原料和 6 种产物的配方，工厂窗口按配方数量换行并调整高度。
- 适配 OrbitalRing 的建造菜单和科技解锁，安装 BuildBarTool 时优先选择上排空位。
- 避免与其他模组待注册的物品、配方和模型占用相同位置或编号。

## 0.1.0

- 添加喷涂工厂，通过上层混带输入原料、下层贯穿传送带输出产物。
- 支持增产与加速模式，按物流运输站输出堆叠科技攒满出货。
- 添加工厂窗口、配方选择、生产统计及配方颜色光效。

## English

### 0.1.1

- Added support for recipes with up to 6 ingredients and 6 products, with slot wrapping and window height adjustment.
- Adapted build-menu placement and technology unlocks for OrbitalRing, preferring free upper-row slots when BuildBarTool is installed.
- Avoided slot and ID conflicts with items, recipes and models queued for registration by other mods.

### 0.1.0

- Added Spray Factory with mixed ingredient input on the upper belt and product output onto the continuous lower belt.
- Added extra-product and speedup modes, with full output stacks based on logistics station output stacking technology.
- Added a factory window, recipe selection, production statistics and recipe-colored effects.
